Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

Clippez runs a small, private tool (the "Clippez Uploader") that publishes short video clips to Clippez's own social media accounts. This policy explains what that tool does with data.

Who uses the app

The Clippez Uploader is used only by Clippez to manage Clippez's own accounts. It is not offered to the public, and no one else signs in to it.

YouTube API Services

The Clippez Uploader uses YouTube API Services to upload videos to the Clippez YouTube channel. By using it, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What data we access

The app requests one permission: to upload videos to the signed-in YouTube account (the youtube.upload scope). It uses this only to upload clips and set their title, description, tags and privacy status. It does not read, collect or store viewers' data, comments, subscriber lists, watch history or any other account data.

How we store it

The only data the app keeps is the sign-in token Google issues for the Clippez account. It is stored on Clippez's own computer, is never shared, and is used only to upload videos. We do not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time from the Google account's security settings: security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. Removing access immediately stops the app from uploading. The stored token can also be deleted from the computer it is kept on.
